# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript; chosen for a lightweight browser game with no installation. No framework preference was supplied.

## Users
Students and graduates aged 18–26 exploring practice and work at Очаково. Employment and practice are available only from age 18. The case prioritizes fourth-year students and graduates.

## Product Purpose
Complete a short playful first shift, discover real production professions, learn company facts, then prepare a candidate application and reach the official careers channel.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Working game, company history, directions, career opportunities, key brands, knowledge questions, candidate fields: full name, contacts, date of birth, preferred role, availability, education institution. Progress may be stored on the device. Personal data must not be represented as sent when no HR integration exists.

## Brand Commitments
Use the supplied bubbly golden mascot as a character reference, adapt clothing to actual company colors. The user explicitly excludes additional branding, merchandise and collateral. Focus on a beautiful lightweight game and show progress previews.

## Evidence on Hand
Original case PDF, student handbook, practice description, company brand book, videos and the report provided by the user. See work/research/requirements.md for extracted requirements.

## Product Principles
Play immediately. Explain professional situations in plain Russian. Mistakes teach and permit another attempt. A game score is not a hiring assessment. Keep the next step honest.
