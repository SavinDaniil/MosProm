# Персонажи: печатная графика

Перерисованы по замечанию заказчика. Встроенный ImageGen, три отдельных редактирования исходных портретов с настоящей прозрачностью. Ограниченная палитра и угловатые формы, книжная линогравюра и перекладная анимация. Готовые WebP: `public/assets/andrey-print.webp`, `elena-print.webp`, `viktor-print.webp`.

Старые портреты убраны из сборки и сохранены в `output/art-archive/`.

## andrey

`andrey-print.webp`

Redraw this character reference as an original sprite for a factory gatehouse detective game. Keep their occupation and broad identifying details, but completely replace the rendering style. Visual language: unusual 1960s Central/Eastern European cut-paper animation combined with carved linocut editorial illustration from a technical magazine. Graphic silhouettes, confidently angular facial planes, asymmetrical distinctive features, sparse dry ink marks and small islands of coarse crosshatching. A deliberately designed face, not a conventionally handsome model. Only five flat spot-ink colors: bone paper, charcoal, dusty sage, iron-oxide burgundy and mustard ochre. Hard-edged printed shadows. Avoid painterly gradients, glossy volume, soft glow, detailed skin, generic concept-art rendering, Disney/Pixar faces, big shiny eyes, pretty portrait aesthetics, and photorealism. Anatomically convincing but subtly awkward and human; restrained wry expression. Front-facing waist-up sprite, full head and shoulders uncropped, body ends at hips, centered on a genuinely transparent background. No background, no decorative frame, no writing or logos. Preserve a solid readable silhouette at a small size. Character: Andrey, a lean male factory equipment engineer in his early thirties. Slightly stooped shoulders, long rectangular face, pronounced hooked nose, small uneven eyes, one eyebrow raised, short rough black hair, ochre hardhat and sage work jacket over a burgundy shirt. Work-worn, attentive, not glamorous. Empty hands relaxed at waist.
